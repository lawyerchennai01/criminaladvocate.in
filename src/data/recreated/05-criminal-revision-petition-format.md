A **criminal revision petition** asks the High Court (or the Sessions Court) to examine the *correctness, legality or propriety* of an order passed by a lower criminal court. It is filed under Sections 397 and 401 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, now Sections 438 and 442 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS). In the Madras High Court such a case is registered as a **Criminal Revision Case (Crl.R.C.)**. This page sets out the usual format and what each part must contain.

## When revision is the right remedy

- The order is **not** an appealable one, or no appeal lies.
- The order is a final or quasi-final one, such as dismissal of a complaint, an order framing charges, an order on discharge, or an order on maintenance by a Magistrate.
- The lower court's order is illegal, perverse, or passed without jurisdiction.

There is a statutory bar on revision against a purely **interlocutory order**. Where the order is interlocutory, the High Court's inherent power (Section 528 BNSS) may be used instead. If a fundamental right is involved, a writ may be appropriate; the existing article on [writ petition versus criminal revision](/writ-petition-vs-criminal-revision-what-to-file-in-madras-high-court-and-when/) helps choose between them.

## Limitation

A revision to the High Court is ordinarily filed within **ninety days** of the order. A delay can be condoned on a petition showing sufficient cause, supported by an affidavit.

## Standard format

**1. Cause title**

> IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT MADRAS
> (Criminal Revisional Jurisdiction)
> Crl.R.C. No. ______ of 2026
> *Name of petitioner* … Petitioner
> Vs.
> *Name of respondent / State rep. by its Public Prosecutor* … Respondent

**2. Heading of the petition**

A short line: "Criminal Revision Case filed under Sections 438 and 442 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (corresponding to Sections 397 and 401 of the Code of Criminal Procedure), praying to call for the records in C.C. / Crl.M.P. No. ___ on the file of the ___ and set aside the order dated ___."

**3. Synopsis and list of dates**

A table of important dates: the date of the offence, the FIR, the charge sheet, the impugned order and the filing date.

**4. Grounds**

Numbered paragraphs, each stating one legal or factual error in the impugned order, for example the court ignored a document on record, applied the wrong legal test, or exceeded its jurisdiction. Keep each ground specific and refer to the page of the records.

**5. Prayer**

The relief sought, for instance: "to set aside the order dated ___ passed by the ___ in ___ and to direct ___."

**6. Interim relief (if needed)**

A separate petition (Crl.M.P.) for stay or suspension of the order, with a short affidavit explaining urgency.

**7. Verifying affidavit and annexures**

- Certified copy of the impugned order.
- Copies of the FIR, charge sheet and other documents relied on.
- Vakalatnama, and court fee as applicable.
- Delay condonation petition, if the revision is filed after ninety days.

## Points that decide the outcome

- **Revisional power is limited.** The High Court does not re-appreciate evidence like an appeal; it looks for illegality, impropriety or perversity.
- **Notice to the other side.** The High Court generally hears the Public Prosecutor and the complainant before passing a final order that prejudices them.
- **Do not leave out the vital facts.** An incomplete record or a misleading statement can harm a petition.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between an appeal and a revision?
An appeal is a statutory right to have the whole case reviewed. Revision is a supervisory power limited to correcting jurisdictional or legal errors.

### Can I file a revision against an order granting or refusing bail?
Bail orders are generally challenged through bail applications or cancellation petitions rather than revision, since the law has its own remedies for them.

### Where do I file a revision against a Magistrate's order in Chennai?
Revision lies to the Sessions Court or the High Court, depending on the order, with the High Court having concurrent power.

### How long does a revision take?
There is no fixed period. It depends on the High Court's roster and whether interim relief is needed.

### Is a stay automatic when a revision is filed?
No. A stay has to be applied for and is granted only where the court is satisfied that it is necessary.
